Verify Garment

Once a manufacturer has registered a garment, you can verify it. This sets isVerified = true in GarmentRegistry and allows the Authentication contract to confirm it as authentic.

Register New Garment

Register a new garment on the blockchain. Once registered, supply chain stakeholders can begin logging updates.

Log Supply Chain Update

Log the current status and location of a garment as it moves through the supply chain. Each update is permanently recorded on the blockchain.
